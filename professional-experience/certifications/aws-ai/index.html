Amazon Web Services · Foundational certification

AWS Certified AI Practitioner

Completed: January 30, 2026

  • AI & machine learning
  • Cloud
Verify credentialIssuer documentation

Overview

This foundational credential focuses on recognizing AI and machine-learning concepts, choosing suitable approaches for business needs, and understanding how AWS services support AI solutions. Its AIF-C01 guide covers the AI/ML lifecycle and use cases, generative-AI capabilities and limits, foundation-model application design and evaluation, prompt engineering, responsible-AI considerations, and security, compliance, and governance. The target exam role is oriented toward using AI/ML offerings rather than building models or deploying AI infrastructure.

Syllabus and skills

  • AI/ML concepts, use cases, lifecycle, and outcome metrics
  • Generative AI fundamentals, model limits, business fit, and AWS infrastructure
  • Foundation-model selection, RAG, prompting, customization, and evaluation
  • Responsible AI, fairness, explainability, and transparency
  • AI system security, privacy, governance, and compliance
